# Delivery

Standard delivery takes 2 to 4 working days and is free for orders over 50 euros; below that it costs 4.90 euros. Express delivery arrives the next working day when ordered before 14:00, for 9.90 euros.

Every order gets a tracking link by email as soon as it leaves the warehouse. If the tracking has not changed for 3 working days, the parcel may be delayed at the carrier: contact support and we open an investigation with the carrier.

A parcel counts as late when it has not arrived 2 working days after the latest delivery date in the confirmation email. For a late parcel we refund the delivery fee automatically, and after 10 working days we treat it as lost and send a replacement or a full refund, as you choose.

If you are not home, the carrier leaves the parcel with a neighbour or at the nearest pickup point, and leaves a card saying where. Parcels wait at a pickup point for 7 days before they are returned to us, and the refund for a returned parcel excludes the delivery fee.

We deliver to every country in the European Union. Orders to Norway, Switzerland and the United Kingdom may be charged customs duties on arrival, which the carrier collects.
