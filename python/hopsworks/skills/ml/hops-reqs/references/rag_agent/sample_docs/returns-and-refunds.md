# Returns and refunds

Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery. The item must be unused and in its original packaging, with all accessories. Personalised items, gift cards and opened software cannot be returned.

To start a return, open Orders in your account, choose the order and press Return item. You will get a prepaid label by email within an hour. Drop the parcel at any partner post office; the tracking number on the label shows its progress.

Refunds go back to the original payment method. Once the warehouse receives the return, it is checked within 2 working days and the refund is issued the same day. Card refunds take 3 to 5 working days to appear on a statement; PayPal refunds appear within 24 hours.

If an item arrives damaged or faulty, report it within 7 days of delivery with a photo. We send a replacement at no cost, or refund the item including the original shipping fee, and you do not need to return a damaged item worth less than 20 euros.

A partial refund is issued when a returned item is missing parts or shows signs of use. The amount depends on the condition, and the support team explains the deduction in the refund email.
